Three sessions into Covington's 2026–2027 Mayor's Academy, and it keeps getting
more real. Carolann Baughman — the mayor's executive assistant — sent over a
calendar invite this week: I'm shadowing Mayor Washington on Tuesday, October 27th,
8:00 AM, at City Hall — moved up from the 8:45 in the invite below, so I can join
him at Holmes High School after.

![Calendar invite for "Jerry Lockard – Shadow the Mayor," Tuesday, October 27, 8:45–9:15 AM, Covington City Hall, 20 W. Pike Street](/SHADOW-DAY-INVITE.png)

That's the whole point of the Academy, really — not just learning how the city
works in a classroom, but standing next to the people who run it and watching how
the day actually goes. I'm grateful to everyone in this administration who's been
welcoming since I moved to Covington. More after the 27th.
